## What I am building

I am developing **OpenProof** to help turn fragmented documents into a structured, source-linked record under human review. **TruthX Engine** is the structuring engine behind the application. The **Registered Probative Object (RPO)** is the draft record format presented in the public specification.

The first product pilot is **OpenProof Legal**: preparing a reviewable documentary record while preserving contradictions, missing information and explicit reservations. Broader applications in crisis reconstruction and decision traceability remain a development direction, not a claim of released products.

The public example is educational: its discrepancy is already written. The supported checker compares a JSON record with a retained reference; it does not ingest your PDFs or discover contradictions. The complete application and TruthX Engine remain private. Integrity comparison does not prove that a statement is true, that a source is authentic or that a record has legal validity.

## My contribution

I define the product problem, workflow and acceptance requirements: how a reviewer finds the source, keeps an unresolved question visible and records responsibility for a decision. My role connects product direction with operational implementation, including work supported by AI tools. The public repository makes specific design choices and their limits examinable.

This profile distinguishes the public material you can inspect from capabilities still being piloted. Repository activity alone is not evidence of production readiness or customer adoption.
